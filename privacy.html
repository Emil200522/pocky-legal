Pocky

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Pocky is a personal money management app. Your financial life is private, and we built Pocky that way. We never connect to your bank, we never sell your data, and we never show you ads. This policy explains exactly what we collect and why.

1. Who we are

Pocky ("we", "us") is the developer of the Pocky mobile application. If you have any question about this policy or about your data, you can reach us at pocky.app1@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details. Your email address and an encrypted password, or the identifier provided by a sign-in provider if you use one.
  • Financial entries. Amounts, categories, descriptions and dates for the transactions you record, plus savings goals, budgets, and the cash, investment and debt figures you enter for net worth tracking.
  • Onboarding answers. Optional details such as age range and occupation, used to tailor your plan.
  • Preferences. Currency, language, notification settings and similar choices.

Information created as you use Pocky

  • Progress data such as level, experience points, coin balance, streaks, completed habits and completed lessons.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the app reliably, such as device type, operating system version and app version.

What we never collect

  • We do not connect to your bank, credit cards, or any external financial institution.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • We do not store your payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

3. How we use your data

We use your information only to operate Pocky. Specifically:

  • To calculate your balances, insights, coins, streaks and goal progress.
  • To personalize your plan and the guidance you see.
  • To send the reminders and summaries you have enabled.
  • To keep your account secure and to prevent abuse.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Who processes your data

We rely on a small number of service providers to run Pocky. They process data on our behalf and are bound by their own agreements and privacy commitments.

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles account authentication. Your app data is stored there.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status and entitlements.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play process all payments and billing.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to do so, or where it is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

5. Where your data is stored

Our servers are located in the United States. If you use Pocky from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed there. By using Pocky you consent to this transfer.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest by our database provider. Your session token is stored on your device using the operating system's secure storage, the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore. Access controls are enforced at the database level so that your records are only ever readable by your own account.

No system is perfectly secure. We work to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your personal data and financial records are permanently deleted. Some records may be retained for a limited period where required by law or for fraud prevention.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction. You can view and edit your information at any time inside the app.
  • Deletion. You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings. This is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Notifications. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your device settings at any time.
  • Requests. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to request a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

9. Subscriptions

Pocky is a paid subscription app. Billing is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and is subject to their terms and privacy policies. We receive only your subscription status, never your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

10. Children

Pocky is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, or under 16 where a higher age applies by local law. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will notify you in the app. Continued use of Pocky after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns: pocky.app1@gmail.com. We respond to all requests within 30 days.
