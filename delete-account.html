Delete your Pocky account

Pocky: Budget & Money Tracker, by Emil Ashurov. Last updated: October 12, 2026

You can delete your Pocky account and all the data linked to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Pocky and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm and tap the delete button.

Your account and data are deleted right away and you are signed out.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't open the app, email pocky.app1@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my Pocky account". We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We complete deletion requests within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, password login and display name.
  • Your profile and onboarding answers, such as date of birth, income estimate, age group and occupation.
  • All financial entries: transactions, income, expenses, recurring items, budgets, categories, savings goals and net worth figures.
  • Your progress: coins, streaks, XP, levels, habits, lessons and weekly insights.
  • Your app settings and preferences stored on our servers.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records. Google Play (or Apple) and our subscription provider RevenueCat keep records of subscription purchases as required for billing, tax and fraud prevention, under their own retention policies. These records don't include your Pocky financial entries.
  • Backups and logs. Copies in routine server backups and technical logs are removed automatically within 30 days.
  • Anonymous statistics. Aggregated usage statistics that can't identify you may be kept.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription

If you subscribe to Pocky Premium, cancel it separately so you aren't charged again. On Android, open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Pocky > Cancel. On iPhone, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual transactions, recurring items, goals and categories inside the app at any time. For any other request, email pocky.app1@gmail.com.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
